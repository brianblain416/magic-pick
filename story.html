Building the band in a laptop

Every song lives in Ableton Live: one big session per act, with each song laid end to end the way it plays on stage. Each song has a two-bar count-in and a Loop at the end, so the band can vamp while the scene finishes. The guitars come in as MIDI, one string per channel, so a synth player can pick up any part.

The guide vocals are Brian's. The characters' voices were built on top of them: Brian sang every part, and each take was turned into Robbie, Melissa or Clive with AI voice tools, then tuned and mixed back under his own guide. The spoken dialogue was generated straight from the script in those same three voices. These are rehearsal voices, placeholders until real actors take the parts.

A rehearsal room on the web

The Magic Pick Player is how the cast and band learn the show without being in the same room. It plays each act straight through, with or without the dialogue, at full mix or band only. Players can sing along with a cast member's take, record a guitar solo over a song's Loop, or download the MIDI for their part.

Anyone signed in can comment on a lyric or a line of dialogue, or suggest new wording. The director picks the changes, and they go back into the Ableton sessions, so the script and the music stay one document.
