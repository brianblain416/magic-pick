The Magic Pick

A musical that amazingly foreshadowed the impact of AI on music today.

In this “guitar musical” the three principals play guitar and trade solos as well as singing, acting and even dancing.

It opens with Robbie, forlorn and drunk, bemoaning his fate playing in a cover band at a run-down Toronto club and yearning for the wife and kid he left behind in Nova Scotia. She appears backstage with a burned-out, eccentric-looking veteran guitarist who wants to pass along the “Magic Pick”, a device that adds a lot of pyrotechnics (sound and visuals) to a guitar performance.

As he plays his way through rock, folk, blues, even moments of disco and classical, Robbie ultimately realizes that he just wants to restore his marriage and get back to playing country music. He is last seen playing in a country band at a barn dance.
